# V4 Design Notes — Based on Busan Lanka Homepage.pdf

**Page 1:** Translucent centered navigation; Sigiriya full-bleed hero and distinctive serif headline; saffron booking CTA and transparent secondary CTA; floating search bar; masonry-like destination gallery (large Sigiriya, Kandy and Golden Beaches).

**Page 2:** Cream background with three service cards and wide fourth card; green Kandy-photo why-us banner and three frosted-glass trust cards.

**Page 3:** Rounded sunset-magenta-to-blue call to action and minimal black footer with live-admin contact details.

Existing CMS, tour packages, Gemini AI UI, WhatsApp enquiries, QR tour and language selector are not part of the supplied PDF visual pages; they are kept as additional functional website sections below the PDF-inspired sections rather than removed.

The V3.5 animated elephant, waterfall, bridge and marine background story scenes were intentionally removed. No scrolling animation framework is required.
